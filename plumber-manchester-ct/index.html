Manchester utility and permit notes

Manchester's Water and Sewer Department operates the town's water and wastewater treatment facilities. Its 24/7 emergency line is (860) 647-3111 and covers sewer backups, sewer main breaks, emergency water shutoffs and water main breaks. Its main number for questions is (860) 647-6050.

If every fixture in the house is backing up, call the town line first to learn whether the problem is in the public main. If the town says the main is clear, the blockage is in your private line and we can help. The Town of Manchester's main number is (860) 647-5235 for permit questions.

Plumbing work in Manchester follows the same state code as East Hartford: the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). The town permit office confirms what your job needs.

Winter, water heaters and what the cold does

Manchester winters are hard on plumbing. Pipes in unheated garages, crawl spaces and exterior walls freeze when the cold lasts, and the first sign is often a trickle where there should be a flow. If you suspect a frozen pipe, open the faucet slightly, keep the heat on and call before the pipe splits. A frozen pipe that has not yet burst can often be thawed safely.

Water heaters work harder in cold months because incoming water is colder. A tank that was marginal in summer may fail in January. Signs include rust-colored hot water, popping sounds, a puddle at the base and pilot trouble. Replacing the tank before it floods the basement is cheaper than cleaning up after.
