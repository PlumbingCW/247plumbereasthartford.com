A commercial corridor with homes close by

This page describes a corridor and not a residential subdivision. The town's Silver Lane redevelopment plan focuses on the stretch from Mercer Avenue to Forbes Street and calls it an eclectic area with a mix of residential, retail, office, industrial and institutional uses.

Landmarks along the road include Silver Lane Plaza at 808 to 850 Silver Lane, Charter Oak Mall at 940 Silver Lane and the former Showcase Cinemas site, which the town bought in 2019 and which has been proposed for apartments. Rentschler Field and the Pratt and Whitney grounds sit nearby, and Interstate 84 is a short drive away.

Why the corridor is changing

The town has published several studies of Silver Lane, including a 2020 revitalization plan and a 2022 redevelopment plan. They describe older retail space, mixed property conditions and efforts to reduce excess retail space.

For a plumber, change means a steady flow of tenant fit-outs, reopened suites and conversions. Each one tends to raise the same questions. What size is the water service, where are the cleanouts, and which fixtures and drains were already in the space?

Planning plumbing for a retail or restaurant suite

A suite that sat empty for a while may have fixtures that have not been used in months. Dry traps can let sewer gas into the space, and old supply valves may stick. Flush every fixture and look under every sink before the opening date, not on it.

Hot water is the next thing to size. A staff sink, a mop sink and a public restroom do not draw the same load as a household, so tell the plumber how many people use the space on a busy day. A tank that is too small shows up as lukewarm water at the worst moment.

Questions to settle before a commercial plumbing visit

Having these answers ready saves time.

  • Who is responsible?Check the lease to see whether the landlord or the tenant owns a given pipe.
  • Where are the shutoffs and cleanouts?Note the main water valve and any drain cleanouts, inside or outside.
  • What is the hours window?Tell us when the space is closed so work does not interrupt customers.
  • Is a permit needed?Ask the East Hartford Department of Inspections and Permits at (860) 291-7340.

Utilities and nearby homes

MDC delivers water and sewer service across East Hartford. For a commercial tenant the useful split is this. The main under the street is MDC's, and the service line into the building belongs to the property owner, so a lease that makes the tenant maintain the plumbing may or may not cover it.

The MDC Command Center is at (860) 278-7850, press 1. Homes on the streets off Silver Lane use the same services, and homeowners can use this page too. Shoppers also use the Buckland Hills retail area across the town line, and we cover Manchester as well. Call 811 before digging along the corridor.
