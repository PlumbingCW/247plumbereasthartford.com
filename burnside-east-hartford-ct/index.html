Plumber in Burnside, East Hartford, CT

Burnside is the part of East Hartford that grows out along Burnside Avenue, State Route 44, east of the Main Street town center. Homes here sit close to a busy mixed-use street, so plumbing calls often start with a drain, a water heater or a leak inside the house.

Where Burnside is and how this page defines it

East Hartford does not publish a formal boundary for Burnside, so this page uses Burnside Avenue as its anchor. The avenue meets Main Street near the town hall, where it marks one end of the Downtown Main Street Historic District. East of there it runs through the part of town people call Burnside.

Two documented landmarks help place the area. The town convened a Burnside Church Street Brownfields Revitalization Advisory Committee, whose study area runs south from Burnside Avenue along Church Street and Scotland Road to Roberts Street. The Mayberry Village neighborhood sits just south of the avenue. If your address is on or near those streets, this page is written for you.

What the housing around Burnside Avenue tends to need

The town has not published a housing survey for Burnside. One neighborhood data profile describes many homes built between 1940 and 1969, with small two to four unit buildings making up a noticeable share. Treat that as a general picture and not a diagnosis of your house.

Age alone tells us little about the pipes inside. We look at what is actually there. In a house of that era a plumber checks the supply lines, the shutoff valves, the water heater and the drain connections in the basement before suggesting anything bigger. In a small multi-unit building the first question is which valve controls which unit, so that one leak does not shut off everyone.

Common reasons Burnside homeowners call

One drain is slow

A single sink, tub or shower that drains slowly usually has a local clog. Drain cleaning clears it without opening anything up.

Several fixtures back up

When a toilet gurgles and a tub fills with gray water, the clog is further along the line. A camera inspection shows where.

No hot water or a leaking tank

A cold shower or a puddle at the base of the tank points to water heater repair or replacement.

Water stains or a high bill

A stain on a ceiling or a bill that jumps without a reason can mean a hidden leak. Leak detection finds it before walls are opened.

Who owns the sewer pipe in Burnside

The Metropolitan District Commission provides water and sewer service in East Hartford. The sewer main in the street belongs to MDC, and the pipe from the house to that main, the lateral, is the homeowner's responsibility.

If every drain in the house is backing up, call the MDC Command Center at (860) 278-7850 and press 1 to rule out a main blockage. If MDC says the main is clear, the problem is on your side. Burnside Avenue also carries a lot of traffic, so call 811 before anyone digs near the street.

What to do before you call

These steps take a few minutes and make the call faster.

  • Find the main shutoff.Know where water enters the house so you can stop a leak at once. In a multi-unit building, ask which valve serves your unit.
  • Note which fixtures are affected.One fixture points to a local clog. Several fixtures point to the main drain.
  • Stop running water.If drains are backing up, hold off on laundry and dishes until the line is clear.
  • Photograph what you see.A picture of a stain, a tag on the water heater or a wet spot helps us advise you over the phone.
