Who we are

East Hartford 24/7 Plumbing is a plumbing dispatch and service company for East Hartford, Connecticut. We answer the phone around the clock, ask what is happening and send a licensed plumber. Our address is 1209 Main St, East Hartford, CT 06108.

We handle emergency calls such as burst pipes, sewer backups, leaking water heaters and boiler failures, and scheduled work such as installs, repiping and sump pump replacement. We serve East Hartford and the nearby towns of Manchester, Glastonbury, South Windsor, Hartford and West Hartford.

How we work

These are the standards we hold ourselves to on every call.

  • Plain answersWe say what the problem is and what the fix involves before work starts.
  • Safety firstWe tell you how to stop water or gas right away, before we arrive.
  • Licensed workLicensed and insured plumbers do the work. You can look up a Connecticut plumbing license at elicense.ct.gov.

Our approach to the local area

East Hartford runs along the Connecticut River, with the Hockanum River crossing the north of town, and that geography shapes the calls we get. Wet basements and sump pumps matter near the water. Older homes and mixed pipe matter in established streets. Commercial calls come from Main Street, Silver Lane and Burnside Avenue.

See our service list or call the 24/7 line at (860) 397-8103 to talk to someone now.

Emergency calls and scheduled work

Emergency calls are the reason the line is staffed overnight. A burst pipe, a sewer backup, a water heater that is flooding a utility room and a boiler that quit on a freezing night cannot wait until Monday. We take those first, ask a few questions, and tell you how to limit the damage while a plumber drives to you.

Scheduled work follows a calmer path. Water heater installs, repiping, fixture replacements, sump pump swaps and camera inspections are booked at a time that suits you, with the scope and the price explained first. Both kinds of work come from the same licensed plumbers, so the quality does not depend on the hour.

What a call looks like

When you call, we ask for the address, what you see and whether water is still running. If you can close a valve safely, we tell you which one. If gas or electricity is involved, we tell you to leave the area first. Then we send the plumber closest to you and keep you informed.

On arrival the plumber finds the cause before anything is cut or replaced, explains the options in plain words and gives you the price. You decide. If the problem is outside plumbing, for example a foundation crack or an electrical fault, we say so instead of selling you a repair that will not help.

Homes, landlords and businesses

East Hartford has single-family streets, two- and three-family buildings and commercial strips along Main Street, Silver Lane and Burnside Avenue. Each has its own pattern of plumbing trouble. Older buildings share stacks and risers, businesses have grease lines and floor drains, and newer homes have tankless heaters and finished basements.

Landlords and property managers can call for a tenant, but we ask who approves the work and who gives access. See commercial plumbing for what we do for shops, restaurants and rental buildings.

Local knowledge that saves time

Some of the most useful information is local. MDC supplies water and sewer service in town, and its Command Center at (860) 278-7850, press 1, takes reports of main-line backups. The East Hartford Department of Inspections and Permits at (860) 291-7340 can say whether a job needs a permit. The state lets you check a plumber's license at elicense.ct.gov.

We share these contacts on our pages because a homeowner who knows who owns which pipe makes a faster, cheaper decision. When a problem belongs to MDC, we tell you to call MDC. When it belongs to you, we fix it.
