What a no-heat call involves

Start with the simple checks. Is the thermostat set to heat and calling for it? Is the boiler switch on? Is there power and fuel? Is the pressure gauge in the normal range, which for many residential systems is roughly 12 to 15 psi cold? If those are fine, the problem is in the boiler or the distribution.

A plumber checks for a tripped limit, a failed circulator pump, stuck zone valves, trapped air in the system, a low water level or an ignition problem. Safety comes first. If there is any sign of gas or carbon monoxide, leave the building and call emergency services.

Common boiler problems

Cold radiators

Air in the system, a failed circulator or a closed valve can stop hot water reaching them. Bleeding a radiator may help, but a pump failure needs a plumber.

Low or high pressure

A leak, a failed expansion tank or a stuck fill valve changes system pressure.

Short cycling

The boiler turns on and off quickly. Causes include an oversized unit, bad sensor or blocked flue. It wastes fuel and wears parts.

Leaks and drips

A leak at a valve, relief valve or the boiler body needs attention. A leak from the body often means replacement.

Repair or replace

A boiler can last a long time with maintenance, but repeated repairs on an old unit add up. We tell you if the cost of the repair approaches the value of a new boiler. Replacement is a planned project with sizing, venting and piping changes, and we can discuss it after the emergency.

Hot water from an indirect tank off the boiler is a different system from a standalone tank heater. For tank problems, see water heater repair. For burst heating pipes, see burst pipe repair.

East Hartford note: homes, fuel and permits

Many East Hartford homes heat with hydronic baseboard or radiators fed by gas or oil boilers. For a gas odor, call 911 and your gas utility. Fuel type affects who must work on the burner, so tell us what your boiler burns.

Replacing a boiler normally involves a permit. Ask the East Hartford Department of Inspections and Permits at (860) 291-7340. Oil and gas burner work may require a separately licensed trade, and we tell you when that applies.

Boiler piping, relief valves and backflow protection on the fill line are regulated by the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and the mechanical rules of the Connecticut code. The 2022 code adopts the 2021 IPC.

What affects the cost of boiler repair

The part and the time of the call set the price.

Failed partA circulator or zone valve costs less than a heat exchanger.
Boiler ageOlder units may need parts that are hard to find.
Fuel typeGas and oil systems have different service needs.
Replacement scopeNew boiler plus piping changes costs more than a repair.
Time of callEmergency visits are available at any hour.

Insurance usually does not cover a worn-out boiler. A burst pipe from a freeze after heat failure can be covered.
