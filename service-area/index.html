Our home base and nearby places

East Hartford is home, and we cover ZIP codes 06108, 06118, 06128 and 06138 across Main Street, Silver Lane, Burnside and the Hockanum area. The five towns above are the larger neighbors we dispatch to every day.

We also take calls from smaller places nearby, such as Bolton and Vernon next to Manchester, Wethersfield, Newington and Rocky Hill south of Hartford, and Bloomfield beside West Hartford. Call the 24/7 line, give the address and we confirm the dispatch.

How we decide where to dispatch

Dispatch follows the road network more than the map. Interstate 84 carries us east to Manchester and west across the Connecticut River to Hartford and West Hartford. Route 2 and the Putnam Bridge connect us to Glastonbury. Route 5 and the streets north of Burnside lead to South Windsor. A bridge closure or a crash on a highway changes the best route, and we plan around it.

The same state code, the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code with the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC), applies in every town we serve. Because every town has its own utility, permit office and housing mix, each town page lists the contacts for that place and the calls we get there. What to have ready when you call from another town

A few details speed up every call. Give the full street address and the nearest cross street, because navigation apps sometimes place rural or new addresses oddly. Say what you see, such as water coming through a ceiling or a drain bubbling, and whether the water is still flowing. Tell us if you rent, because we may need the owner or manager to approve the work.

If you can reach the main water valve safely, close it before you call. It is usually where the water line enters the basement or utility room. If you have a gas appliance involved, do not relight it yourself. We will walk you through the safe steps while the plumber is on the way.

Winter calls need an extra word. Cold snaps cause frozen and burst pipes, and boilers fail when they are needed most. Keep the heat on, open cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls and let a faucet drip if a freeze is forecast. These small steps often save a call, and when they do not, we are a phone call away.
