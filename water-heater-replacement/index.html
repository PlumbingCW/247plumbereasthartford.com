What happens when a tank gives out

Steel tanks fail slowly from the inside. The anode rod sacrifices itself to corrosion until it is gone, then the tank wall rusts through at the weakest point. Water appears at the base, and by the time a puddle shows, the tank cannot be saved.

That is why a leaking tank is treated as an emergency. A small seep can turn into a flood of forty or fifty gallons when the shell finally opens. Shutting off the supply buys time, and the replacement visit does the rest.

How a replacement visit runs

Confirm and size

The plumber checks the failed unit, the fuel type, the venting and the household hot water demand to select a matching tank.

Quote before work

You get a clear price before the old heater is touched.

Remove and install

The old heater is drained and removed. The new one is set, connected, vented and fitted with a relief valve and discharge pipe.

Test and walk-through

The plumber fires the unit, checks combustion or element operation, confirms no leaks and explains the controls.

East Hartford note: permit, code and license

The Town of East Hartford lists plumbing systems such as water heaters among the work that usually requires a permit, and it says the owner, not the contractor, is responsible for violations. Call the Department of Inspections and Permits at (860) 291-7340 to confirm for your job.

The 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code, which sets rules for relief valves, discharge piping and venting. Ask any plumber to show you a P-1 license on the state eLicense lookup before the old heater comes out.

Relief valve discharge, drain pans in finished areas and venting are covered by the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and the fuel gas rules of the Connecticut code. The 2022 code adopts the 2021 IPC.

What to do before we arrive

If the tank is leaking, take these steps first.

  • Shut the cold supplyThe valve is on the pipe above the heater.
  • Turn off power or gasUse the breaker or the gas valve on the unit.
  • Do not touch hot waterWater from a leaking tank can scald.
  • Open a hot tap upstairsThis relieves pressure while the tank drains.

What to clear before the plumber arrives

A little prep speeds up the visit.

  • Open access to the heaterMove boxes and stored items so the tech can reach the tank and the shutoff.
  • Locate the shutoffsKnow where the cold water valve and the gas or electric shutoff sit.
  • Clear the path outThe old tank is heavy. Keep the route to the exit free of obstacles.
  • Note the detailsWrite down the model, capacity and fuel type from the label if you can reach it.
