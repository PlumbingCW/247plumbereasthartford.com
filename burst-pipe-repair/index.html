The first ten minutes decide the damage

Find the main shutoff valve. In most homes it is where the water line enters, often in the basement near the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops. If the valve is stuck or the leak is upstream of it, call us and the dispatcher will talk you through the next step.

Then open a cold tap on the lowest floor to drain the pipes, and turn off the water heater power or gas so the tank does not run dry. Move belongings out of the wet area and photograph everything before cleanup. Your insurer will want those pictures.

Before we arrive

These steps limit the damage while the plumber is on the way.

Stop the water

Close the main shutoff. If you do not know where it is, find it now so you can show the tech.

Kill power to wet areas

If water is near outlets or the panel, shut off the breaker only if you can reach it dry. Otherwise stay clear.

Drain and contain

Open the lowest faucet. Use towels and buckets, and lift furniture off the floor.

Document

Take photos and video of the damage and of the burst section before anything is moved.

Frozen pipes: thaw safely or wait for the plumber

Connecticut winters freeze pipes in unheated spaces such as exterior walls, garages and crawlspaces. A frozen pipe often shows up first as a faucet that only trickles. The pipe may not have burst yet, and thawing it slowly can prevent a break.

Open the faucet so water can flow as the ice melts. Warm the pipe with a hair dryer, never an open flame. If you cannot find the frozen section, or you hear water running behind a wall once it thaws, call. A pipe that cracked under ice pressure often leaks only after it warms, which is the moment a flood begins.

What bursts, and why

Frozen copper or PEX

Ice expands inside the pipe and splits it at the weakest point, usually along a short seam.

Aging galvanized steel

Corrosion thins the wall until a pressure surge opens it. These pipes often fail in several places over time. See repiping.

Failed fittings or supply lines

A washing machine hose or a corroded connector can burst without any freezing involved.

Pressure spikes

A water hammer or a faulty pressure reducing valve can stress older pipes beyond their limits.

East Hartford note: permits, licenses and who is responsible

The East Hartford Department of Inspections and Permits lists plumbing systems among the work that usually needs a building permit, and its guidance says the current owner, not the contractor, is always responsible for violations. Call (860) 291-7340 to ask whether a given pipe repair needs one.

Check the plumber's license before work starts. A Connecticut P-1 license covers all plumbing and piping work for a contractor, and it can be looked up on the state's eLicense page.

Replacement pipe and fittings must be approved for potable water under the International Plumbing Code (IPC). The 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 IPC, so repairs use listed materials rather than temporary patches.
