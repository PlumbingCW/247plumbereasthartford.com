Emergency Faucet and Sink Repair in East Hartford, CT

Emergency faucet and sink repair in East Hartford, CT is for a tap that will not shut off, a spraying supply line or a shutoff valve that does nothing. Our licensed plumbers repair or replace faucets, cartridges and valves, and the 24/7 line takes the call when you cannot stop the water.

If a faucet will not shut off, close the small valves under the sink. If those fail, close the main water valve for the house.

Under the sink, the fast fix

Every sink has two small valves under it, one hot and one cold. They stop water to that fixture without cutting the whole house. Turn each clockwise until it stops. If a valve is stiff, do not force it with a wrench, because a corroded stem can snap. In that case close the main shutoff and call us.

A valve that turns but does not stop the water is worn inside. That is a frequent finding in older homes, and it is a good moment to replace the valve while the water is off.

Common faucet and sink calls

Dripping spout

A worn cartridge, washer or seat lets water pass when the faucet is closed. The fix is a new internal part, not a new faucet in most cases.

Leak under the sink

Check the supply lines and drain connections. Braided supply lines can burst at the nut, and P-trap nuts loosen over time.

Handle that will not stop

A broken stem or cartridge keeps the valve open. The repair needs the supply shut off first.

Low flow from one tap

Mineral buildup in an aerator or a partly closed shutoff reduces flow. A cleaning or new cartridge restores it.

Fixture replacement versus parts

A faucet that is ten or fifteen years old may have discontinued parts, and chasing them can cost more than a new faucet. We tell you which way is cheaper and which will last. If you already bought a faucet, we install it. If not, we can guide you on mounting hole counts and kitchen versus bathroom styles.

Kitchen sinks add disposal and dishwasher connections, which is why a faucet swap sometimes reveals a loose drain fitting. Callers who also have a garbage disposal in the same cabinet should see the disposal repair page, since the two often fail together.

East Hartford note: the water you are fixing for

Mineral buildup on fixtures depends on the water coming into the house. MDC publishes an annual Water Quality Report and runs a lab you can call with questions at (860) 278-7850, extension 3901. If your aerators clog quickly, ask what the report says about hardness before you choose new fixtures.

For any job where a valve is replaced behind a wall, confirm whether the East Hartford permit office wants a permit by calling (860) 291-7340.

Fixture connections, shutoffs and backflow protection at faucets and hose bibs fall under the International Plumbing Code (IPC). The 2022 Connecticut State Building Code adopts the 2021 IPC.
