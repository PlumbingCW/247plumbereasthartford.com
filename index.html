Emergency plumber in East Hartford, CT

East Hartford 24/7 Plumbing is an emergency plumber for East Hartford, Connecticut, and the towns around it. When a pipe bursts at midnight or a water heater lets go on a Sunday, you need someone who picks up. Our line is open 24/7, and a licensed plumber is sent to your door.

We cover ZIP codes 06108, 06118, 06128 and 06138, from the shops and homes along Main Street and Silver Lane to Burnside Avenue and the Hockanum area in the north of town. Our core work is drains, sewers, leaks, water heaters, boilers and fixtures, and we also take commercial calls.

Many people here keep irregular hours, with employers such as Pratt & Whitney and Goodwin University in town, so an after-hours plumber matters. We answer early, late and overnight, and we say plainly what the problem is and what it will take to fix it.

What East Hartford homeowners should know

MDC supplies water and sewer service in East Hartford. The sewer main belongs to MDC and the lateral from your house to the main belongs to you. If a backup affects several homes, MDC's Command Center takes calls at (860) 278-7850, press 1. If only your home is affected, call our emergency plumber line.

Low-lying properties near the Connecticut River and the Hockanum River can see higher groundwater in wet seasons, which makes sump pumps and basement drains matter. See sump pump service. For permit questions, the East Hartford Department of Inspections and Permits is at (860) 291-7340. Plumbing work follows the 2022 Connecticut State Building Code, which adopts the 2021 International Plumbing Code (IPC). Call 811 before you dig.

Great River Park and the riverfront draw people to the west side of town, and Route 2, I-84 and I-384 connect East Hartford to Manchester and Glastonbury, which is why we can reach those towns quickly. Rentschler Field and the stadium bring traffic on game days, and we plan dispatch around it.
